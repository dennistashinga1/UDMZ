Organizational Profile

Registered Private Voluntary Organisation under the Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare.

Universe Disaster Mitigation Zimbabwe (UDMZ) is a wholly owned indigenous non-profit making organisation. Our core business is to alleviate and manage natural and human disasters in Zimbabwe and across the planet.

We seek to transform humankind to pursue their purpose through training, workshops, transfer of knowledge, skills, and God’s word. We stand active against all enemies of peace, love, and security, mitigating threats to humans, animals, and the environment.

Our Methodology: UDMZ operates in partnership with all relevant stakeholders inclusive of internal and external individuals, communities, local government authorities, parastatals, private companies, and various government departments.

Our Objectives

  • Focus on disaster alleviation, public education, and awareness.
  • Establish a Rehabilitation centre for disaster survivors.
  • Provide emergency aid and empowerment to survivors.
  • Actively oppose all forms of violence to humanity and the environment.

Our Pillars of Restoration

UDMZ seeks to restore and protect the earth and its inhabitants through four core areas of management.

🌍 Human Domination

Humankind was given the stewardship and authority to protect and manage the environment at creation. We work to eradicate factors that advance environmental degradation and natural/human disasters, fulfilling our God-given mandate.

🕊️ Soul Management

Addressing vast emotional suffering emanating from abuse and worldly challenges. We seek to establish rehabilitation & legal centres for:

  • Mentally disturbed patients
  • Drug abuse survivors
  • Survivors of abuse and violence
  • Disaster and accident survivors
  • Vulnerable groups (elderly, street kids)

🌱 Environment Management

We are deeply committed to the physical restoration and protection of mother earth, specifically focusing on:

  • Restoration and protection of forests
  • Protection of all water bodies
  • Proper waste management & recycling
  • Infrastructure development

🛡️ Peace, Security & Disasters

Breaking threats to peace and security through sustainable livelihood projects, humanitarian aid, and establishing quick reaction teams for disasters in partnership with state security, EMA, and local authorities.

Establishing the UDMZ Centre

Our grand vision is to establish a unified Disaster Alleviation and Management Centre. This will serve as a base for training, resource storage, community preparedness programs, and emergency response operations.

This space will uniquely house relevant departments in one area—including social development, police, legal practitioners, EMA, Parks and Wildlife Management, local government, and private security—to holistically and rapidly address issues brought by vulnerable communities.
